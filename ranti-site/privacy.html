Privacy policy

Effective 8 October 2026 · applies to the Ranti Android app and ranti-ng.vercel.app
In one line: your people, dates, notes and reminders live on your phone. Ranti's server is only used if you share your birthday link or switch on automatic birthday emails for someone. No ads, no tracking, no selling of data.

Who we are

Ranti is a birthday and reminder app made and owned by Olaoluwa Michael (legal name Micheal Aroja Olaoluwa), an independent developer in Akure, Nigeria. He is the data controller for the small amount of data described below. Contact for anything about privacy: michaelolaoluwagab@gmail.com.

This policy is written to meet the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and, for users in the EU or UK, the GDPR.

Data that stays on your phone

Everything you add (names, birthdays and other dates, relationships, phone numbers, emails, notes, gift ideas, plan checkmarks, reminders and settings) is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot see it. It works with no internet connection.

  • Contacts (optional). If you tap "Import from contacts" and allow access, Ranti reads your address book on the phone to find saved birthdays and to let you pick friends to ask for theirs. Your contact list is never uploaded.
  • Backups. "Back up" creates a file that you choose where to keep (for example WhatsApp to yourself, Google Drive or email). The file holds your whole list and your birthday-link key, so keep it private. Android's own device backup may also include Ranti's data in your Google account backup if you have it switched on; that is handled by Google under your Google account settings.

Data that uses Ranti's server (only if you use these features)

FeatureWhat is sentWhy
Your birthday linkA random link code, a secret key (stored only as a one-way hash), and the first name you set in the app.So friends see "Add your birthday for <name>" and their answers reach only your phone.
Friends who use your linkThe name, birthday and, if they choose, phone number and email that your friend types on the web page.Held on the server so your phone can collect them and add them to your list (deleted after 90 days at most).
Automatic birthday email (per person, off by default)That person's name, email address, birthday (day and month) and your custom message (web links are removed), plus your first name, shown as "<name> via Ranti".So the email can be sent on their birthday, even if your phone is off.

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). It is stored with our service providers: Vercel (hosting of ranti-ng.vercel.app), Supabase (database) and Brevo (sending the birthday emails). They process it only to provide these services. We do not sell or share data for advertising. These providers run servers outside Nigeria (for example in the EU and the USA); they protect data under their own security and data-processing terms, and we send them only what each feature needs.

Legal basis. Data stays on your phone unless you switch on one of these features, so we process it because you asked us to (performing the service you chose) and, for the people you add, on the basis of a legitimate interest in remembering and celebrating their birthday. Friends who type their own details on your link page give them by choice for that purpose.

Birthday emails. The email comes from Ranti's sending address with your name on it. If the person replies, the reply reaches the Ranti team, not you; we do not use it for anything else.

People who receive an automatic birthday email can unsubscribe with one click in that email; we keep their email address on an opt-out list only so they are never emailed again.

What Ranti does not do

  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no location access, no account or sign-in.
  • No selling or renting of personal data.
  • Contacts are never uploaded.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications: to show your reminders.
  • Alarms & reminders: so reminders ring at the exact time you set.
  • Contacts (optional): only for "Import from contacts", as described above.
  • Internet: only for the birthday link and automatic emails.
  • Run at startup: to set your reminders again after the phone restarts.

How long we keep server data

  • Friends' entries from your birthday link: until your phone has had time to collect them, and never more than 90 days.
  • Automatic-email list: replaced every time your phone syncs; a person is removed when you switch their email off or delete them.
  • Send log (one line per person per year, to prevent double emails): deleted after the following year.
  • Your birthday link: deleted with everything linked to it if your phone has not been in touch for 12 months (for example after you uninstall the app).
  • Opt-out list: kept as long as Ranti sends email, so people who unsubscribed are never emailed again.

Deleting your data

  • On your phone: delete a person or reminder in the app, or uninstall Ranti to remove everything stored on the device.
  • On the server: in the app, open Settings → Privacy → Delete my cloud data. This deletes your birthday link, friends' entries waiting on the server, your automatic-email list and its send log, immediately.
  • Or email michaelolaoluwagab@gmail.com with the subject "Ranti data deletion" and your birthday-link code (Settings → Share my birthday link). We delete it within 30 days, usually much sooner.
  • If someone else added you (you typed your birthday on a friend's link, or you get birthday emails): unsubscribe with the link in the email, or email us with your name and email address and we will delete what the server holds about you.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data the server holds about you, to correct it, to delete it, to get a copy, or to object to its use. Email michaelolaoluwagab@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng) or, in the EU or UK, to your local data protection authority.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Nobody outside can read, change or delete anything in the database: only Ranti's own server can, and it checks each phone's secret key, so nobody can see another person's list. We will tell affected users and the regulator if a breach ever puts their data at risk, as the law requires.

Children

Ranti is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
